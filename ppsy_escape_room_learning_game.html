Escape Dr. D. Lirious's Mind Maze

Welcome, Foundation Student! Eccentric professor Dr. D. Lirious has locked you inside his diagnostic testing facility. To unlock each room and win your freedom, you must examine clinical evidence and accurately diagnose psychological disorders!

Learning Objectives

  • Differentiate key clinical presentations of Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), Bipolar Disorder, Persistent Depressive Disorder (Dysthymia), and Schizophrenia.
  • Distinguish positive symptoms (hallucinations, delusions) from negative symptoms (flat affect, avolition).
  • Apply diagnostic reasoning to complex clinical scenarios and case studies.

How to Play & Controls

Mouse & Keyboard

Click on highlighted room objects (files, whiteboards, terminals) to inspect evidence. Use number keys 1-4 or mouse to select diagnostic answers.

Mobile & Touch

Tap glowing props directly on screen. Responsive buttons are sized for easy touch navigation across all mobile devices.

Scoring Mechanics & Rules

  • 100 Points per correct diagnostic choice on the first attempt.
  • 50 Points for second attempt after reading feedback explanations.
  • Untimed Mode grants full learning opportunities with zero time pressure.
